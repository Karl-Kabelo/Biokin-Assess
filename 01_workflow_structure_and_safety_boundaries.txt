BIOKIN ASSESS - WORKFLOW STRUCTURE AND SAFETY BOUNDARIES

1. Patient Layer

Purpose:
The patient gives information. No clinical interpretation happens here.

Patient provides:
- assessment category
- body map selection
- symptom type
- pain score
- yes/no screening answers
- free-text symptom story
- booking preference

Assessment categories:
- Pain/Injury
- Rehabilitation
- Chronic Health
- Sports Performance
- General Fitness

2. AI Intake Structuring Layer

Purpose:
The AI only organises the data. It does not diagnose, treat, or make clinical decisions.

AI outputs:
- clean patient-reported summary
- anatomical region selected by the patient
- surrounding anatomical structures from standard reference anatomy
- common clinical examination categories for that region
- positive screening responses
- unanswered screening responses

AI must NOT output:
- diagnosis
- likely condition
- clinical impression
- treatment plan
- referral decision
- exercise prescription
- emergency judgement

Safe wording:
Use:
- selected anatomical region
- nearby anatomical structures
- common examination categories
- positive screening responses
- for clinician review only

Avoid:
- likely affected area
- possible diagnosis
- suspected condition
- suggested treatment
- red flag confirmed
- likely L5-S1 involvement

3. Patient View

Purpose:
Simple, plain-language summary for the patient.

Patient sees:
- what they reported
- selected areas
- pain score
- free-text symptom description
- general safety advice
- clear disclaimer
- booking calendar

Patient disclaimer:
This is not a medical diagnosis. Your responses have been organised for review by a qualified professional.

4. Intern Dashboard

Purpose:
The intern receives a clean case file and completes human clinical reasoning.

Intern sees:
- raw patient data
- AI-structured intake
- anatomical reference layer
- common exam categories
- booking details

Intern completes:
- subjective notes
- objective assessment plan
- clinical reasoning
- proposed intervention strategy

Important:
Intern notes are human-generated, not AI-generated.

5. Supervisor Dashboard

Purpose:
The supervisor reviews intern work and provides oversight.

Supervisor sees:
- case queue
- patient intake
- AI-structured reference layer
- intern notes
- booking details
- audit trail

Supervisor actions:
- Approve
- Request Changes
- Escalate / Refer
- Add guidance comments

Suggested case statuses:
- New
- Awaiting Intern Review
- Draft Plan Ready
- Supervisor Review Needed
- Approved
- Changes Requested
- Escalated

6. Audit Trail

Purpose:
Track every important action.

Audit trail records:
- patient submitted assessment
- AI intake structuring completed
- patient booked consultation
- intern saved notes
- intern sent case for supervisor review
- supervisor approved/requested changes/escalated
- comments added
